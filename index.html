ABOUT LEXICURA

Errors are easier to fix
when they are understood.

Traditional lexical analyzers are excellent at identifying invalid tokens, but their feedback is often limited to a technical error message. LexiCura adds a reasoning layer around lexical analysis.

01

Detect

Locate suspicious tokens and lexical inconsistencies.

02

Understand

03

Repair

04

Validate

Run the analysis again after the correction.
